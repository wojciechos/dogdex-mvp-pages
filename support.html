DogDex support

Need help with DogDex?

DogDex is a free dog collecting camera app. The first release focuses on catching a dog, saving a local card, and browsing your Dex.

Common Checks

  • Camera permission must be enabled before live capture can work.
  • Your saved DogDex collection is local to your device in the current MVP.
  • No account or subscription is required.
