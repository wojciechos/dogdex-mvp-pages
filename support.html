FaunaNote support

Need help with FaunaNote?

FaunaNote is a free animal collecting camera app. Photograph an animal, identify the likely species, and keep the sighting in your private on-device collection.

Common Checks

  • Camera permission must be enabled before live capture can work.
  • Your saved FaunaNote collection stays local to your device.
  • Species identification can be wrong. Do not rely on it for wildlife, safety, or veterinary decisions.
  • No account or subscription is required.
