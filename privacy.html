DogDex Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

DogDex is a free dog-collecting camera app. It does not require an account, does not contain ads, and does not upload your saved DogDex collection to a DogDex server.

Camera, Photos, And AI Breed Guesses

DogDex asks for camera access so you can catch a dog and create a local collectible card. When you capture a photo for an AI breed guess, DogDex sends the photo through a DogDex service hosted by Vercel to Google Vertex AI.

The photo is used to decide whether it shows a dog and, if so, return a likely breed, confidence, and candidate matches. Results can be wrong and are not veterinary, pedigree, or DNA advice. DogDex does not use submitted photos for advertising or tracking and does not train its own model with them.

DogDex does not intentionally create a permanent server-side photo library or write classifier photos to an application database. Vercel processes the HTTPS request and Google processes the image for the AI result. Google documents that published Gemini models cache customer inputs, outputs, and derived data in project-isolated memory for up to 24 hours by default. Google also documents that prompts flagged as suspicious may be stored securely for abuse investigation for up to 90 days unless the applicable Google Cloud account has an approved abuse-logging opt-out. DogDex conservatively assumes these periods apply until a disabled-cache or approved opt-out configuration is verified. Google states that this data is not used to train or fine-tune AI models without the customer's permission or instruction.

A request that has already been processed cannot be recalled, and provider security records may remain under the periods above. DogDex selects processors subject to contractual and privacy protections appropriate to their role and requires them to provide protection consistent with this policy. If a processor cannot provide equivalent protection, DogDex will stop that processing path.

Provider references: Google Cloud retention, Google abuse monitoring, and Vercel Privacy Policy.

Saved Collection And Optional Catch Details

Saved stickers, breed cards, dog names, place labels, notes, and approximate coordinates stay in local app storage on your device. They are not uploaded as collection data. You may add an optional dog name and note to a saved catch.

Location

When you tap Save to Dex, DogDex may request foreground location once. If you allow it, DogDex rounds latitude and longitude to two decimal places before asking Apple's geocoding service for an approximate place label and before saving the coordinate for the private on-device map. Apple may process that approximate coordinate to provide the label and map. If you deny access or location fails, the dog is saved without a place. DogDex does not send the place label or coordinate to its own servers. You can edit or remove the place later.

Feedback And Feature Requests

In version 1.0.1, DogDex prepares a message in your device's email app. You review and choose whether to send it. DogDex does not directly transmit the message or optional reply email from the app. If you send the email, your email provider and DogDex support process it to respond or improve DogDex. Contact DogDex support to request deletion of a message you sent.

Retention And Deletion

DogDex has no user account or cloud copy of your collection. Privacy & Data lets you clear the saved collection and local sticker files. Unsaved camera and sticker-processing files are deleted on retake, cancellation, completion, or screen exit on a best-effort basis.

Local data remains until you delete it in DogDex, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes app storage. Clearing local data does not alter the provider retention periods for already processed classifier requests described above.

Third-Party Services And Content

DogDex uses Apple platform services for distribution, maps, approximate place labels, and local Apple Vision subject lift; Vercel and Google Vertex AI for AI breed guesses; your email provider only when you choose to send a prepared message; and runtime libraries needed to render the app.
