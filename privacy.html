DogDex Privacy Policy

DogDex is a free dog collecting camera app. The current MVP does not require an account, does not include ads, and does not send your DogDex collection to a DogDex server.

Camera And Photos

DogDex asks for camera access so you can catch a dog and create a local collectible card. Camera frames and saved DogDex cards are used inside the app experience.

When you capture a dog, the photo is sent securely to the DogDex breed identification service, where an AI vision model recognizes the breed. Photos are processed only to answer that request: they are not stored on DogDex servers, not used to build profiles, and not used for advertising.

Your saved DogDex collection - stickers, breed cards, dog names, place labels, and notes - stays local on your device and is never uploaded.

Optional Catch Details

You may add optional details to a saved catch, including a dog name, a place label, and a note. These details are stored locally in your DogDex collection.

Location

DogDex can attach an approximate place to a catch, but only when you tap "Add place" and grant the system location permission. The place label and approximate coordinates are stored locally in your collection on your device and are not sent to DogDex servers. You can edit or remove a saved place at any time, and the private map of your own catches lives entirely on your device.

Third-Party Services

DogDex uses Apple platform services for app distribution and TestFlight. The app also includes runtime libraries used to render the mobile experience and animations.

Breed identification runs on Google Cloud (Vertex AI) infrastructure operated for DogDex; photos submitted for identification are not used by DogDex to train models and are not retained after the request completes.

DogDex does not currently include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or a user account backend.
