DogDex Privacy Policy

DogDex is a free dog collecting camera app. The current MVP does not require an account, does not include ads, and does not send your DogDex collection to a DogDex server.

Camera And Photos

DogDex asks for camera access so you can catch a dog and create a local collectible card. Camera frames and saved DogDex cards are used inside the app experience.

The current MVP stores your saved DogDex collection locally on your device. DogDex does not operate a backend service that receives your photos, stickers, dog names, place labels, or notes.

Optional Catch Details

You may add optional details to a saved catch, including a dog name, a place label, and a note. These details are stored locally in your DogDex collection.

Location

DogDex does not currently request device location permission. If a future version adds maps or location-based features, location will be opt-in and documented before release.

Third-Party Services

DogDex uses Apple platform services for app distribution and TestFlight. The app also includes runtime libraries used to render the mobile experience and animations.

DogDex does not currently include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or a user account backend.
